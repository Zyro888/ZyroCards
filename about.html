About ZyroCards

Building the future of virtual payments

Our Mission

ZyroCards exists to make online payments safer, simpler, and free for everyone. We believe you shouldn't pay fees to spend your own money or risk your primary card details on every website.

By providing unlimited free virtual cards with instant controls, we're putting the power back in your hands. No more fraud anxiety. No more surprise charges. Just total control.

Our Story

Founded in 2024 by a team of ZyroCards Inc and security engineers, ZyroCards was born from a simple frustration: why are virtual cards still expensive and complicated in 2026?

Our founders experienced card fraud firsthand after shopping online. Despite using "secure" sites, their card details were stolen and used for $3,000 in unauthorized charges. The bank took weeks to resolve it.

We knew there had to be a better way. If companies can generate unlimited virtual numbers internally, why can't consumers have the same protection for free?

Six months later, ZyroCards launched. Today we serve 250,000+ users across 12 countries and have processed over $180M in secure transactions with zero reported fraud cases.

What Makes Us Different

1. Truly Free Forever

No monthly fees. No activation fees. No per-card fees. No hidden charges. We make money through interchange — a small fee merchants pay Visa/Mastercard — not from you.

2. Bank-Grade Security

  • PCI DSS Level 1: Same security standard as major banks
  • AES-256 Encryption: Military-grade encryption for all data
  • Zero-Knowledge: We never store full card numbers in readable form
  • Instant Freeze: Lock any card in 1 second from your phone
  • Merchant Locks: Tie a card to one merchant only — if stolen, it's useless elsewhere

3. Built for Real People

  • No Credit Check: Anyone 18+ with valid ID can sign up
  • Instant Setup: Card ready in under 2 minutes
  • Human Support: Real people, not bots. Average response: 3 minutes
  • No Dark Patterns: Cancel anytime. No tricks.

Our Values

  • Security First: Every decision starts with "is this safe for users?"
  • Radical Transparency: Clear pricing, clear policies, no fine print
  • User Control: Your money, your rules. We're just the tool
  • Privacy by Design: We collect minimum data needed and never sell it
  • Financial Inclusion: Everyone deserves safe payment tools, regardless of credit score

How We Make Money

ZyroCards is free for users because merchants pay a small fee called "interchange" to Visa/Mastercard on every transaction. We receive a portion of that fee from our issuing bank partners.

We never: Sell your data, charge monthly fees, or show ads in the app. Your trust is worth more than short-term profit.

Security & Compliance

ZyroCards operates under strict regulatory oversight:

  • Partner Banks: All cards issued by FDIC-insured partner banks
  • Compliance: Full KYC/AML compliance, BSA reporting
  • Certifications: PCI DSS Level 1, SOC 2 Type II
  • Insurance: $5M cyber liability insurance policy
  • Audits: Quarterly security audits by third-party firms

Funds are held in pooled accounts at partner banks and are FDIC-insured up to $250,000 per user.
